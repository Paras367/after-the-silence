# AFTER THE SILENCE

**An Independent Indian Women's Safety & Accountability Archive**

> Remembering the cases. Recording the promises. Measuring the change.

---

## About

**After the Silence** is a serious, investigative-style public-interest archive documenting major cases involving violence against women and girls in India.

This is **not a generic awareness website**.

It is designed as a:

- Digital memorial
- Historical record
- Legal-awareness platform
- Case archive
- Reform tracker
- Government accountability resource
- Public-interest research project

The archive examines what happened **after the headlines disappeared** — the institutional failures, investigations, court proceedings, legal reforms, government promises, implementation gaps, and questions that remain unanswered.

The goal is simple:

> **Remember the people. Document the evidence. Examine the response. Measure the change.**

---

## Core Principles

The project follows a strict editorial approach:

- Victims and survivors are treated with dignity.
- Anonymous survivors are never identified.
- Minors are protected from identification.
- Graphic or exploitative material is not used.
- Allegations are not presented as convictions.
- Court findings are distinguished from media reports.
- Government claims are distinguished from verified implementation.
- Statistics are used only when supported by credible sources.
- Ongoing cases are clearly marked as ongoing.
- Disputed historical accounts are explicitly identified as disputed.

### Fact Status

Cases and individual claims may be marked as:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `VERIFIED` | Supported by reliable documentation |
| `REPORTED` | Reported by credible media or organizations |
| `ALLEGED` | An allegation that has not been judicially established |
| `COURT FINDING` | Established through judicial proceedings |
| `DISPUTED` | Conflicting or contested accounts exist |
| `ONGOING` | Investigation or legal proceedings are continuing |

## Phoolan Devi

**The complexity of a life.**

A major historical investigation examining Phoolan Devi's life through the documented history surrounding:

- Childhood
- Child marriage
- Sexual violence
- Social and institutional inequality
- Police and institutional failures
- Her years with dacoit groups
- The Behmai killings
- Surrender
- Imprisonment
- *Bandit Queen* and disputes over her portrayal
- Political career
- Assassination

The project does **not** reduce Phoolan Devi to either a victim or an outlaw.

# Case Structure

Every major case follows a consistent structure:

### 01 — What Happened?

A concise, factual account.

### 02 — Who Was Affected?

Respectful information about victims and survivors.

### 03 — Investigation

Police and investigative developments.

### 04 — Institutional Response

Actions and failures involving relevant institutions.

### 05 — Court Process

Where applicable:

- FIR
- Arrests
- Charges
- Trial
- Verdict
- Appeals
- Final legal status

### 06 — Public Response

Documented protests, campaigns and public discussion.

### 07 — Government Response

Statements, policies, committees, legislation and official actions.

### 08 — What Changed?

Documented legal or institutional reforms.

### 09 — What Did Not Change?

Evidence-based shortcomings, gaps or unresolved issues.

### 10 — Current Status

The latest verified status of the case.

### 11 — Sources

Primary and secondary documentation.

# Promise vs. Reality

One of the central features of the project is an accountability tracker comparing:

**WHAT WAS PROMISED**

with

**WHAT WAS IMPLEMENTED**

and

**WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS**

Example structure:

| Reform / Promise | Announced | Implementation | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Public transport safety | Government / authority announcement | Documented implementation | Official source | `PARTIAL` |
| CCTV requirements | Policy / regulation | Evidence of implementation | Audit / report | `VERIFIED` |
| Police response improvements | Reform proposal | Evidence required | Official data | `UNKNOWN` |

The project must **never automatically label a reform as failed** without credible evidence.

Possible statuses:

- `IMPLEMENTED`
- `PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED`
- `DOCUMENTED FAILURE`
- `ONGOING`
- `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`

# Editorial Standards

The project follows an evidence-first approach.

## We distinguish between:

### **FACT**

Information supported by reliable evidence.

### **ALLEGATION**

A claim made by a party that has not necessarily been established.

### **COURT FINDING**

A conclusion reached through judicial proceedings.

### **REPORTING**

Information published by a credible news organization.

### **ANALYSIS**

Interpretation based on documented evidence.

### **DISPUTED**

Accounts or evidence that conflict.

### **ONGOING**

A matter where investigation or legal proceedings continue.

# Privacy & Survivor Protection

This project takes survivor privacy seriously.

The website must never publish:

- Private addresses
- Phone numbers
- Private medical information
- School information that could identify a protected minor
- Family information that could expose a survivor
- Leaked intimate material
- Private photographs
- Identifying details of anonymous survivors

For protected survivors, use language such as:

> **"A survivor whose identity remains protected."**
